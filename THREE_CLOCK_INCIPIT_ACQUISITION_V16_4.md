# Three-clock incipit acquisition v16.4

Opening authority now requires agreement between the chart's first expected-role onset, the earliest supported transcription-MIDI occurrence, and actual role-stem activity. Audio establishes when the performance starts; chart-to-MIDI matching establishes phrase identity. A bounded transcription lag is corrected only for the validated opening region and is preserved diagnostically as the raw MIDI time.

The root requires at least two independent source windows, proximity to the first chart onset, chart/audio agreement within 750 ms, and transcription lag between 0 and 4 seconds. Later repeated phrases cannot establish the incipit. Controls generated from one window do not count as independent confirmations.
